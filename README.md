# Ruijie Yang · Academic Homepage

Personal academic homepage of Ruijie Yang (杨睿捷), University of Science and Technology of China.

Website: https://gensheep.github.io/

## Publishing

In Settings → Pages, select Deploy from a branch, main, and /(root), then Save.

## Editing

- index.html: biography, research interests, education and email.
- styles.css: visual design and responsive layout.
- script.js: email copying and navigation.
- favicon.svg: website icon.

No build or package installation is required. The time series graphic is a conceptual illustration, not experimental data. No projects or publications are listed.
